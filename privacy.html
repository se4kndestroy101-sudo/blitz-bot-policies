Privacy Policy

BLITZ Discord Bot | Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BLITZ ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores and handles information when users interact with it on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames and server nicknames.
  • Discord server IDs, channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Message activity and message counts for XP and levelling.
  • Experience points, levels, rankings and leaderboard statistics.
  • Virtual currency balances, purchases and transaction records.
  • Moderation records, including warnings, mutes, kicks and bans.
  • Server configurations and Bot preferences.
  • Command usage and interaction information.

The Bot does not need to permanently store message content to provide message-count-based XP. Any additional message content processing must be disclosed if implemented.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and maintain the Bot, including:

  • Tracking user activity and awarding XP.
  • Maintaining levels, rankings and leaderboards.
  • Operating the virtual economy.
  • Providing moderation tools and maintaining moderation records.
  • Recording relevant Bot activity and errors.
  • Saving server configurations and preferences.
  • Preventing abuse, exploitation and unauthorised access.

3. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in databases or other hosting services used to operate the Bot.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by hosting providers, database providers or other services necessary to operate the Bot. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features, maintain server functionality and address security or moderation issues.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how it is used.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal data associated with their Discord account by contacting the Bot developer using the contact details below.

Server administrators may also request deletion of server-specific Bot data.

Some information may be retained where required for legitimate security purposes or legal obligations.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and uses Discord's platform and API. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Bot, its features or its data practices. The latest version will always be available at this webpage.
